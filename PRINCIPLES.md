# Engineering Principles

1. **Production-first.** Every artifact is intended to remain useful in the final platform.
2. **Architecture before implementation.** Important boundaries and trade-offs are documented before they become accidental dependencies.
3. **Fast, but not throwaway.** Rapid prototypes use the same quality and security posture expected of durable services.
4. **Modular by default.** Providers, models, tools, and user experiences are replaceable behind explicit contracts.
5. **Observable and attributable.** Agent actions and retrieved web evidence must be traceable.
6. **Secure by default.** External content is untrusted; credentials, tool access, and network egress are controlled.
7. **Human-centered.** Agent outputs must be understandable and actionable for business users.
8. **Open-source quality.** Documentation, tests, contribution paths, and decisions are part of the product.
