# Quickstart: Topic Research Wiki

1. Create a topic such as **Agentic web**.
2. Open **Research**, ask a plain-language question, choose a source count, and
   submit it. The existing governed workflow runs automatically.
3. Research displays the canonical stored progress. If the workspace shows
   **Needs your decision**, inspect the exact evidence and accept or reject it
   before asking another question in that topic. This does not delete the
   source; it records the existing human review decision.
4. A completed question with changed qualified evidence creates a dated wiki
   revision and a **Latest update** at the top of **Wiki**.
5. When a prior revision exists, use **What changed since the previous
   revision** to read the stored change summary or open that prior revision.
6. Follow a citation to the stored source summary and then to its original URL.
7. Use **Sources** to inspect retained summaries, keywords, trust results, and
   exclusion reasons. Use **Research** for question history.
8. **Studio** is visible but read-only in this release.

Repeated questions reuse the existing run only inside the same topic. A source
that is blocked, unreviewed, inaccessible, insufficient, or unsummarized can
be visible with its reason, but cannot support a wiki claim.
